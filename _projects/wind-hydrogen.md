## Question

How can a hybrid installation that combines wind power with hydrogen production and storage deliver stable energy output, and what limits it in practice?

## Approach

Development of optimisation algorithms for wind–hydrogen hybrid energy production. The project's paper analyses the operational performance and scaling constraints of a real pilot installation.

## Outputs

- An, N., Turp, M., **E. Bayındır**, Demiralay, Z., & Avci, C. Operational Performance and Scaling Constraints of a Wind-Hydrogen Hybrid Energy System: Evidence from a Real Pilot Installation. *Energy Reports*. <span class="muted">Under review</span>
