## Question

How can a hybrid installation that combines wind power with hydrogen production and storage deliver stable energy output, and what limits it in practice?

## Approach

Development of optimisation algorithms for wind–hydrogen hybrid energy production. The project's paper analyses the operational performance and scaling constraints of a real pilot installation.

## What I found

The pilot couples a 900 kW wind turbine at Boğaziçi University's Kilyos Sarıtepe Campus with an electrolyser of about 5 kW, 15 kg of hydrogen storage and a 2 kW fuel cell. Surplus wind energy was available during 65.06% of the analysed period, but the hydrogen subsystem ran only occasionally: hydrogen utilisation exceeded 95%, yet the self-sufficiency ratio stayed below 2%. The limit came from the size mismatch between the turbine and the hydrogen units and from the operating strategy, not from the hydrogen technology itself.
